4D Reel Launcher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026 · 4D Suite

The short version

The 4D Reel Launcher extension collects no personal data, stores nothing, and transmits nothing. Its only network activity is a status check to 127.0.0.1:8765 — your own computer — to see whether your locally-installed 4D Reel studio is running.

What the extension does

  • Reads engine status and your recent project names from the 4D Reel app running on your machine.
  • Opens your local studio dashboard in a browser tab when you click a button.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, cookies, or fingerprinting.
  • No transmission of data to any third party or to 4D Suite servers (none exist).
  • No account, no email, no personal information of any kind.

The 4D Reel app

The extension is a companion to the open-source 4D Reel application, which runs entirely on your computer and connects to AI providers only using API keys you supply yourself. 4D Reel is licensed under MIT; its source is publicly available.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer through the support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
